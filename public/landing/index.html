How an order moves

From “I want this” to delivered, without the back-and-forth.

1

Order comes in

From your storefront, a chat or a walk-in. Your team gets an alert.

2

Customer pays

Through a payment link. You approve the transfer in one tap.

3

Prepared

Pick, pack and print the label. Stock updates by itself.

4

On its way

Log the courier. The customer follows it on their tracking page.

5

Delivered

The customer confirms delivery. If something's wrong, they start a return.

What your customers see

How an order moves

Every order has two sides. Fyll takes care of both.

While your team packs and ships, your customer hears from you at every turn. So you stop getting "Hi, please any update on my order?"

  1. From a DM, your store or your counter, it lands in one list. Your customer has a confirmation before they've closed the chat.

  2. They send the transfer and upload the receipt. You check it and tap approve. No more receipts lost in a chat.

  3. Pick, pack, print the label. Your stock counts itself, and your customer knows their order is on the table.

  4. Add your logistics partner and you're done. Your customer watches the order move without calling you.

  5. They tap to confirm it arrived. Loved it? They leave a review. Something off? They start a return, no back-and-forth.

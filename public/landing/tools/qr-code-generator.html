About this tool

QR code generator

A QR code saves your customer from typing. One scan opens your store, starts a WhatsApp chat with a message already written, or shows your bank details so they can pay.

Print it on packaging inserts, flyers, receipts and price tags, or stick it on your shop counter and market stall. Use it in Instagram stories and WhatsApp status too.

Tip: keep at least 2cm of space around a printed code, and print it no smaller than 2.5cm wide so every phone can read it.

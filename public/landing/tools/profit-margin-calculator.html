About this tool

Profit margin calculator

Many small businesses price by adding a little on top of what they paid. The problem is that packaging, delivery you cover and payment fees quietly eat into that profit.

This calculator adds up your real cost per item first. Then it either suggests a price for the margin you want, or shows the margin you get at the price you already charge.

Margin vs markup: margin is profit as a share of your selling price. Markup is profit as a share of your cost. A 50% markup is only a 33% margin.

It depends on what you sell and how much you spend on marketing, rent and staff. Use the price table to see what each margin means in real money, then pick one that covers all your running costs.

Include any delivery cost you pay yourself, such as free delivery offers. If the customer pays delivery separately, leave it at zero.

Some payment methods take a percentage of each sale. If you only take bank transfers with no charges, leave it at 0%.

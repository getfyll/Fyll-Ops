About this tool

Refund policy generator

A refund policy answers the questions customers ask before they buy: can I return it, how long do I have, and how will I get my money back?

You can set your own rules for change-of-mind returns, choose which items you cannot take back, and decide whether refunds go back by transfer, as store credit or as an exchange.

Whatever you choose, customers who receive faulty or wrong items are still protected under Nigerian consumer law, and this template says so.

Nigerian consumer law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018, protects customers who receive faulty, damaged or wrong items. You can set your own rules for change-of-mind returns, but your policy should not remove those protections.

Pick a window you can manage. Short windows, like 3 to 7 days after delivery, are common for fashion and accessories. Perishables and personal care items are usually excluded.

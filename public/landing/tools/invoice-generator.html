About this tool

Invoice generator

An invoice tells your customer exactly what they are paying for, how much, and where to send the money. It also gives you a record for your books.

This generator is built for how businesses in Nigeria get paid. Add your bank details so customers can pay by transfer, and ask them to use the invoice number as their narration so you can match the payment quickly.

Tip: keep your invoice numbers in order, like INV-0001, INV-0002, so you can find any sale later.

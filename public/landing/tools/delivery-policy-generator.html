About this tool

Delivery policy generator

"How much is delivery to Abuja?" "When will it get to me?" A delivery policy answers these before the customer asks, so you can spend that time packing orders.

Set your processing time, delivery times and starting fees for your city and other states, and add pickup, pay on delivery or international shipping if you offer them.

Tip: promise times you can meet on a busy week, not a quiet one. Customers forgive slow far more easily than late.

It depends on your logistics partner and where the customer is. Same-day or next-day delivery within the same city and 2 to 5 business days to other states are common. Set times you can reliably meet.

Pay on delivery can help first-time customers trust you, but it also leads to more failed deliveries. Many businesses offer it only in their own city, sometimes with a small commitment fee.
